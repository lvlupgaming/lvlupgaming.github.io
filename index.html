ClipForge

ClipForge is a personal desktop tool that turns long videos into short vertical clips and, with the owner's permission, uploads finished clips to the owner's own YouTube channel.

What it does

  • Runs entirely on the owner's own Windows computer.
  • Finds highlight moments in videos, adds captions, and renders short clips.
  • Uploads approved clips to YouTube channels the owner has connected, using the YouTube Data API.

Privacy

ClipForge does not collect, sell or share any personal data. Read the full Privacy Policy.

ClipForge uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
